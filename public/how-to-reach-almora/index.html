Himalayan Hamlet / Almora guide

How to reach Almora and Himalayan Hamlet

The easiest way is a train or bus to Kathgodam or Haldwani, then a taxi up to Almora. Distances are approximate and differ by a few kilometres between sources, so treat them as a guide.

Step by step from Delhi

  1. Take the evening train or bus to Kathgodam or Haldwani. Trains sell out in busy months, so book early.
  2. From the station, take a shared cab (reported at about ₹250 to ₹300 per person) or hire a private taxi to Almora. We can help arrange one (check price).
  3. Kainchi Dham lies on the road from Kathgodam to Almora, so you can stop to visit on the way (check).
  4. Tell us when you are an hour away, and we will guide you to the parking spot.

The last 300 metres

You can drive to the street parking, away from the main road. From there it is a walk of about 300 metres to the house, downhill on the way in. It is easy for most guests. Please tell us beforehand if you have elders, young children or heavy luggage, and we will meet you and help with the bags. We recommend arriving before sunset, as the path is not lit.

Before you set off

  • Message us on WhatsApp for road and weather updates. The hills can see landslides in the monsoon, and we will tell you the latest. your usual advice?
  • Fill up on fuel and withdraw cash in Haldwani or Almora town (check).
