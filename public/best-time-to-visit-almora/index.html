Best time to visit Almora, month by month

Almora sits at about 1,650 m, so it is cool even in summer and cold in winter. The right month depends on what you want: clear snow views, easy weather, or quiet.

MonthsWhat to expectGood for
March to MayMild to warm days, cool evenings, rhododendrons in bloom (check)Families, first-time visitors, walks and temple visits
JuneWarmest month, hazier views, tourist rush on weekends (check)Escaping plains heat; book early
July to SeptemberMonsoon: green hills, heavy rain, some road disruption (your advice on travelling in the rains)Quiet, budget-friendly stays; travel with flexible dates
October and NovemberClear skies and the sharpest Himalayan views (check)Photography, Nanda Devi views, festival season
December to FebruaryCold, crisp mornings, occasional snow in the higher hills (how often does snow reach Almora town?)Winter stays, bonfires, peaceful weeks
