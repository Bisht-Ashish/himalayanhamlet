Things to do in Almora

Almora is a base for slow days: temples on the ridges, forest walks, and day trips to Binsar and Jageshwar. We live here, so these are the places we send our own guests, with distances from Himalayan Hamlet.

PlaceFrom the homestayWhy go
Kasar Devi Temple3 kmA hilltop temple known for meditation, for its Van Allen belt magnetic anomaly, and for the well-known people who meditated here (add the names you want to mention, e.g. Swami Vivekananda). Valley and Himalayan views, and a calm spot at sunrise.
Nanda Devi Temple4 kmThe main temple of Kumaon's goddess, in the heart of Almora's main market. Visit it while you explore the local bazaar, its sweets and shops.
Chitai Golu Devta Temple6 kmShrine of the local deity Golu Devta, known for the thousands of bells left by devotees.
Binsar Wildlife Sanctuary12 kmOak and rhododendron forest, birdwatching and a wide Himalayan viewpoint; a good full-day trip.
Katarmal Sun Temple20 kmA 9th-century sun temple with finely carved stone panels, worth the drive and the quiet.
Jageshwar Dham33 kmA cluster of more than 100 old stone temples set in deodar forest.
Kainchi Dhamabout 40 km (check)The ashram of Neem Karoli Baba on the Nainital road, now very popular with visitors from India and abroad. It gets crowded at weekends and during its annual fair in June (check).

A simple plan for three days

  1. Day 1: Settle in, watch the sunset from the balcony and take an evening walk. your local tip
  2. Day 2: Kasar Devi at sunrise, then Nanda Devi Temple and the Almora bazaar. Try the local bal mithai and singori (check).
  3. Day 3: Katarmal Sun Temple and Jageshwar by car, then back for a home-cooked dinner.
