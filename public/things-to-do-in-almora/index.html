Himalayan Hamlet / Almora guide

Almora as we see it

These are the places we send our own guests. Distances are from the house.

Close to the house

  • Kasar Devi Temple, 3 km. A hilltop temple known for meditation, for its Van Allen belt magnetic anomaly, and for the well-known people who meditated here (add the names you want to mention). Valley and Himalayan views, and a calm place at sunrise.
  • Nanda Devi Temple, 4 km. The main temple of Kumaon's goddess, in the heart of the main market. Visit it while you explore the local bazaar, its sweets and shops.
  • Chitai Golu Devta Temple, 6 km. The shrine of the local deity Golu Devta, known for the thousands of bells left by devotees.

Day trips

  • Binsar Wildlife Sanctuary, 12 km. Oak and rhododendron forest, birdwatching and a wide Himalayan viewpoint. A good full-day trip.
  • Katarmal Sun Temple, 20 km. A 9th-century sun temple with finely carved stone panels, worth the drive and the quiet.
  • Jageshwar Dham, 33 km. More than 100 old stone temples set in deodar forest. One guest called it the high point of their trip.
