Almora trip tips: what to pack and what to expect

A few practical things we tell every guest before they arrive, so the stay is easy.

What to pack

  • Layers: even in summer the evenings are cool. Warm jackets from October to March.
  • Comfortable shoes with grip for the walk to the house and for temple steps.
  • A torch for evening walks; paths are not lit.
  • Motion-sickness tablets if anyone gets queasy on hill roads. The last stretch from Kathgodam is winding.
  • Cash for small shops. Most places take UPI, but network can be patchy.

Arriving at the house

  • Park on the street, then walk about 300 metres to the house (downhill on the way in?).
  • Tell us when you are an hour away and we will guide you.
  • your note: help with luggage for elderly guests or children

Good to know

  • Wi-Fi is available at the house (check speed and mobile network).
  • Hill roads can be slow after rain; keep a buffer on travel day.
  • Plan your route with how to reach Almora.
