What to eat and buy in Almora

Almora is known for its sweets, simple Kumaoni food and local crafts. Most of it is in the main market, near the Nanda Devi Temple.

Sweets to try

  • Bal mithai: a brown fudge made from roasted khoya, coated in tiny sugar balls. Almora's best-known sweet.
  • Singori: a khoya sweet wrapped in a maalu leaf.

Kumaoni food

  • Bhatt ki churkani (black soybean curry), aloo ke gutke, mandua (finger millet) roti, jhangora kheer and bhang ki chutney are classic Kumaoni dishes. (check which of these you serve or can arrange)

What to buy

  • Handmade copper utensils from Almora's traditional coppersmiths (the Tamta community) (check)
  • Woollens, shawls and Kumaoni caps
  • Aipan art, a traditional Kumaoni folk art done in red and white
